the most popular cars in the Middle East


The automotive market in the region is currently defined by a two-way competition: the continued dominance of Toyota and the rapid rise of Chinese brands

Toyota

Toyota remains the #1 best-selling car manufacturer across the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) markets .
In Saudi Arabia alone, Toyota held a dominant 28% market share in the first half of 2025 .
This enduring popularity is driven by legendary reliability, strong resale value, and an extensive after-sales service network .
Key models include the Land Cruiser, Hilux, Camry, and Yaris .
